Federated Learning with Differential Privacy
Privacy-Preserving ML

A formal FedAvg implementation from first principles, coordinating distributed training across heterogeneous clients without centralizing raw data. Integrated differential privacy mechanisms to quantify privacy budgets under non-IID data splits. Measured accuracy degradation against privacy strength (ε) to evaluate real-world trade-offs rather than theoretical claims.

Federated Rounds: 15 · Clients: 4 · Data: Non-IID
Baseline Accuracy: 0.9825
DP-enabled (ε = 0.7): Accuracy: 0.8596

The 12-point degradation quantifies the empirical privacy–utility frontier; stronger privacy (smaller ε) demands steeper accuracy loss.
