Federated Learning with Differential Privacy
Distributed & Private ML

A FedAvg implementation, built from the mathematical formulation up, coordinating training across multiple simulated clients without ever centralizing raw data. Includes a differential privacy mechanism and non-IID data handling — plus honest measurement of what privacy actually costs.
